🚀 Getting Started

New to DraftlyCV? Start here to learn the basics.

What is DraftlyCV?

DraftlyCV is a free, modern web-based resume builder designed to help you create professional resumes quickly and easily. With two stunning design options and intuitive editing tools, you can craft a resume that stands out.

Key Features:

  • Two Modern Designs: Choose between Sidebar layout (Design 1) or Timeline layout (Design 2)
  • Real-time Preview: See your changes instantly as you type
  • Photo Upload: Add a professional photo to your resume
  • Theme Colors: 5 beautiful color themes to match your style
  • Export & Import: Save your work as JSON and continue later
  • PDF Download: Generate high-quality PDF resumes
  • Mobile-Friendly: Edit your resume on any device

✓ Best of all: DraftlyCV works entirely in your browser. No sign-up required, and your data stays private on your device!

Creating Your First Resume

Follow these simple steps to create your first resume with DraftlyCV:

  1. From the Homepage: Click the "Create Your Resume" button
  2. Choose Your Design: Select between Design 1 (Sidebar) or Design 2 (Timeline)
  3. Fill in Your Information: Start with personal details, then add your experience, education, skills, and certificates
  4. Customize Colors: Pick a theme color that matches your personal brand
  5. Preview & Download: Review your resume in real-time and download as PDF when ready

💡 Pro Tip: Your progress is automatically saved in your browser, so you can close the page and come back anytime!

Choosing Between Design 1 and Design 2

DraftlyCV offers two distinct resume designs to suit different preferences and industries:

Design 1: Sidebar Layout

  • Style: Professional sidebar with contact info and skills on the left
  • Best For: Traditional industries, corporate roles, academic positions
  • Features: Clean separation of content, easy-to-scan layout

Design 2: Timeline Layout

  • Style: Modern timeline design with gradient header and centered layout
  • Best For: Creative fields, startups, Gen Z professionals
  • Features: Contemporary look, visual timeline dots, gradient accents

⚠️ Important: Each design saves data separately. If you switch designs, you'll need to re-enter your information or import a previously exported draft.

✏️ Building Your Resume

Learn how to add and manage all sections of your resume.

Personal Information & Photo

The personal information section is the foundation of your resume. It appears at the top and includes your contact details.

Required Fields:

  • Full Name: Your complete name as you want it to appear professionally
  • Job Title: Your current role or the position you're seeking (e.g., "Senior Software Engineer")
  • Email: A professional email address
  • Phone: Best contact number with country code if relevant

Optional Fields:

  • Address: City and country (full address not necessary for privacy)
  • Website/Portfolio: LinkedIn profile, personal website, or portfolio URL
  • Professional Summary: 2-3 sentences highlighting your key strengths and career goals

Adding a Photo:

  1. Click the "Choose Photo" button in the Personal Information section
  2. Select a professional headshot (JPEG or PNG format)
  3. The photo will appear in both the form preview and final resume
  4. To remove: Click "Remove Photo" button

📸 Photo Tips: Use a high-quality headshot with good lighting, professional attire, and a neutral background. Smile naturally and ensure the photo is recent!

Work Experience

The Work Experience section showcases your professional history and achievements.

Adding Experience:

  1. Click the "+ Add Experience" button
  2. Fill in the following fields:
    • Job Title: Your role (e.g., "Marketing Manager")
    • Company: Organization name
    • Duration: Time period (e.g., "Jan 2020 - Present" or "2018 - 2020")
    • Description: Key responsibilities and achievements
  3. Click "Save" to add the experience
  4. Repeat for all relevant positions

Managing Experience Entries:

  • Edit: Click the blue "Edit" button on any entry
  • Delete: Click the red "Remove" button
  • Order: Add experiences in reverse chronological order (most recent first)

✍️ Writing Tips: Use action verbs (led, developed, managed), quantify achievements (increased sales by 30%), and focus on results rather than just duties.

Education

The Education section displays your academic qualifications.

Adding Education:

  1. Click "+ Add Education" button
  2. Enter:
    • Degree: Full degree name (e.g., "Bachelor of Science in Computer Science")
    • School: University or institution name
    • Year: Graduation year or range (e.g., "2016 - 2020")
    • Description: GPA, honors, relevant coursework (optional)
  3. Click "Save"

Best Practices:

  • List in reverse chronological order (most recent first)
  • Include GPA if it's 3.5 or higher
  • Mention honors, scholarships, or awards
  • For recent graduates: include relevant coursework and projects

Skills & Proficiency Levels

The Skills section highlights your technical and soft skills with visual proficiency indicators.

Adding Skills:

  1. Click "+ Add Skill" button
  2. Enter the skill name (e.g., "JavaScript", "Project Management", "Adobe Photoshop")
  3. Set proficiency level using the slider (0-100%):
    • 20-40%: Beginner/Basic
    • 40-60%: Intermediate
    • 60-80%: Advanced
    • 80-100%: Expert/Master
  4. Click "Save"

Skill Categories:

  • Technical Skills: Programming languages, software, tools
  • Soft Skills: Communication, leadership, teamwork
  • Languages: English (Native), Spanish (Fluent), etc.

⚠️ Be Honest: Only include skills you can confidently demonstrate. Inflating proficiency levels can backfire in interviews!

Certificates & Achievements

Showcase your professional certifications, courses, and achievements.

Adding Certificates:

  1. Click "+ Add Certificate" button
  2. Fill in:
    • Certificate Name: Full certification title
    • Issuer: Organization that issued it (e.g., "Google", "Coursera", "AWS")
    • Year: Year obtained or "2020 - 2025" for validity period
    • Description: Brief details about what the certification covers (optional)
  3. Click "Save"

What to Include:

  • Professional certifications (PMP, CPA, AWS Certified, etc.)
  • Online courses from recognized platforms
  • Industry-specific licenses
  • Awards and honors
  • Published papers or patents

💼 Stand Out: Certifications show commitment to professional development and can set you apart from other candidates!

Editing and Removing Entries

All sections (Experience, Education, Skills, Certificates) work the same way:

To Edit:

  1. Find the entry you want to modify
  2. Click the blue "Edit" button
  3. Make your changes in the form fields
  4. Click "Save" to apply changes

To Remove:

  1. Find the entry you want to delete
  2. Click the red "Remove" button
  3. The entry is immediately deleted (no undo available)

⚠️ Tip: Before removing content, consider exporting a draft first. You can always import it later if you change your mind!

🎨 Customization

Make your resume uniquely yours with themes and color options.

Theme Colors

DraftlyCV offers 5 beautiful gradient color themes to personalize your resume.

Available Themes:

  1. Purple: Professional and creative (Purple to Violet gradient)
  2. Blue (Default): Trust and reliability (Blue to Cyan gradient)
  3. Green: Growth and harmony (Green to Teal gradient)
  4. Teal: Modern and fresh (Teal to Lime gradient)
  5. Red: Bold and energetic (Red to Orange gradient)

How to Change Theme:

  1. Scroll to the "Choose Theme Color" section in the editor
  2. Click on any color circle to apply that theme
  3. The preview updates instantly showing:
    • Header gradient background
    • Timeline dots and lines (Design 2)
    • Skill proficiency bars
    • Section accents
  4. Your theme choice is automatically saved

🎨 Color Psychology: Blue suggests trust (finance, healthcare), Green for sustainability/growth, Purple for creativity, Red for energy/passion!

Design Switching

You can switch between Design 1 and Design 2 at any time.

To Switch Designs:

  1. Click "← Back to Theme Selector" at the top of the editor
  2. Choose a different design from the theme selector page
  3. You'll be taken to the new design's editor

⚠️ Important Note: Each design maintains separate data. Your Design 1 data won't automatically appear in Design 2. To transfer data:

  1. Export your draft from Design 1
  2. Switch to Design 2
  3. Import the draft file

💾 Export & Save

Save your work and download professional PDF resumes.

Export Draft (JSON)

Export your resume data as a JSON file to save locally or transfer between designs.

How to Export:

  1. Click the "Export Draft" button at the top of the editor
  2. A JSON file will download automatically (e.g., "DraftlyCV_Resume.json")
  3. Save this file somewhere safe (Desktop, Google Drive, etc.)

When to Export:

  • Before clearing your browser data or cache
  • To create backups of different resume versions
  • To transfer resume data between Design 1 and Design 2
  • To work on multiple resumes (export each separately)

💡 Best Practice: Export your draft regularly! Think of it as "Save As" for your resume. Keep versions like "Resume_Software_Job.json" and "Resume_Manager_Job.json"

Upload Draft (Import)

Load a previously exported resume draft back into the editor.

How to Import:

  1. Click the "Upload Draft" button
  2. Browse and select your JSON file
  3. The file loads instantly and populates all fields
  4. Your previous data is replaced with the imported data

Common Uses:

  • Restoring a backup
  • Working on a different computer
  • Switching between resume versions for different jobs
  • Transferring data from Design 1 to Design 2 (or vice versa)

⚠️ Warning: Importing a draft will replace your current resume data. Export your current work first if you want to keep it!

Download PDF

Generate a professional, high-quality PDF of your resume ready for applications.

How to Download PDF:

  1. Ensure all your information is complete and accurate
  2. Choose your preferred theme color
  3. Click the "Download PDF" button
  4. The PDF generates automatically and downloads to your device
  5. File is named "resume.pdf" by default

PDF Features:

  • Professional quality suitable for printing
  • All colors and gradients properly rendered
  • Optimized A4/Letter size format
  • No watermarks or branding
  • Photos included if uploaded

Before Downloading:

  • ✓ Review all sections for typos and errors
  • ✓ Check that all dates are correct
  • ✓ Verify contact information is current
  • ✓ Ensure your photo looks professional (if included)
  • ✓ Preview how the theme color looks

📄 Pro Tip: Save different PDF versions for different jobs (e.g., "Resume_Google.pdf", "Resume_Amazon.pdf") after tailoring content!

📱 Mobile Usage

DraftlyCV works great on smartphones and tablets!

Using DraftlyCV on Mobile Devices

The editor is fully responsive and optimized for mobile use.

Mobile Features:

  • Touch-Friendly: All buttons and inputs work perfectly with touch
  • Adaptive Layout: Editor and preview stack vertically on small screens
  • Preview Toggle: Special "View Preview" button to see your resume
  • Auto-Save: Your work saves automatically just like on desktop

Mobile Workflow:

  1. Fill in your information in the left panel (this is your editing area)
  2. Click "View Preview" button to see how your resume looks
  3. Click "✕ Close Preview" to return to editing
  4. Switch back and forth as needed
  5. Export, import, and download PDF all work on mobile!

💡 Mobile Tip: For best experience on phones, use landscape mode when reviewing your final resume preview!

Mobile Preview Toggle

On mobile devices, the editor and preview don't fit side-by-side, so we provide a toggle button.

How It Works:

  • By default, you see the editing panel
  • Click "View Preview" to switch to preview mode
  • In preview mode, the "✕ Close Preview" button appears at the top-right
  • Click it to return to the editing panel

✓ Best Practice: Make a few changes, then preview. Repeat this cycle rather than constantly switching!

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Professional advice to create standout resumes.

Resume Writing Best Practices

Content Guidelines:

  • Keep It Concise: Aim for 1 page if you have <10 years experience, max 2 pages otherwise
  • Tailor for Each Job: Customize your summary and experience for each application
  • Use Action Verbs: Start bullets with words like "Led", "Developed", "Managed", "Increased"
  • Quantify Achievements: Include numbers (e.g., "Increased sales by 30%", "Managed team of 12")
  • Focus on Results: Emphasize what you achieved, not just what you did
  • Recent First: Always list experience in reverse chronological order

What NOT to Include:

  • ❌ Personal information like age, marital status, religion
  • ❌ Unprofessional email addresses (use firstname.lastname@email.com)
  • ❌ References (add "Available upon request" if needed)
  • ❌ Salary information or expectations
  • ❌ Hobbies unless directly relevant to the job

Design & Formatting Tips

Visual Appeal:

  • Choose Colors Wisely: Match theme to industry (Blue for corporate, Purple for creative)
  • Professional Photo: Use a high-quality headshot with good lighting
  • Consistent Dates: Use same format throughout (e.g., "Jan 2020 - Dec 2022")
  • White Space: Don't overcrowd - leave breathing room between sections

ATS-Friendly Tips:

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to scan resumes:

  • Use standard section titles (Work Experience, Education, Skills)
  • Include relevant keywords from the job description
  • Avoid graphics-heavy designs (both our designs work well with ATS)
  • Submit as PDF to preserve formatting

Data Management Tips

Organizing Multiple Resumes:

  • Version Control: Export drafts with descriptive names (e.g., "Resume_SoftwareEng_2024.json")
  • Master Resume: Keep one comprehensive version with everything, then create tailored versions
  • Regular Backups: Export monthly or before major browser updates
  • Cloud Storage: Store exported JSON files in Google Drive/Dropbox for access anywhere

Privacy & Security:

  • All data stays in your browser (localStorage) - nothing sent to servers
  • Exported JSON files contain all your data - store securely
  • Clearing browser data will erase your saved resume (export first!)
  • Incognito/Private browsing won't save your data

Troubleshooting Common Issues

My data disappeared!

  • Cause: Browser cache was cleared or using incognito mode
  • Solution: Import your last exported JSON file
  • Prevention: Export drafts regularly!

PDF looks different from preview

  • Cause: Browser rendering differences
  • Solution: Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
  • Check: Ensure all content fits properly before downloading

Photo not uploading

  • Check: File must be JPG or PNG format
  • Size: Very large files (>10MB) may have issues - compress first
  • Format: Some HEIC or WebP formats may need conversion

Can't see mobile preview button

  • Cause: Screen is wide enough for split view
  • Expected: Button only appears on screens narrower than 968px
  • Resize: Make your browser window smaller to test mobile view

💬 Still having issues? Try refreshing the page or clearing just the DraftlyCV data using "Clear All Data" button, then import your draft again.
